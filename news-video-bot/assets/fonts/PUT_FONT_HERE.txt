Is folder mein ek Hindi (Devanagari) font file daalo — video ke text-card pe
Hindi headlines sahi dikhne ke liye ye zaroori hai (default font Hindi text
show nahi kar payega, boxes/blank dikhega).

Free option (recommended):
  Google Fonts -> "Noto Sans Devanagari" -> Bold weight download karo
  -> file ko yahan rakho aur naam do: NotoSansDevanagari-Bold.ttf
  (ya config.yaml mein video.font_path update kar do agar naam alag rakha)

Audio (TTS) is font se independent hai — wo hamesha sahi Hindi bolega,
chahe font missing ho. Sirf on-screen text ke liye font chahiye.
